Questions and answers

Who do you work with?

Creative, social, media and integrated agencies, and in-house marketing and brand teams at mid-size and large companies.

What does it cost?

An Opportunity Session is £600. AI audits start from £2,500, team workshops from £1,500 and builds from £3,500. All prices exclude VAT, and every project has a fixed scope and price agreed before it starts. See services and pricing.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. We're based in London and work with teams anywhere, remotely or on site.

Which AI tools do you use?

We're tool-agnostic. We recommend and build with whatever fits your team, your budget and the tools you already pay for.

Is our information kept confidential?

Yes. Client work stays confidential, and we're happy to sign an NDA before any detailed conversation.

What happens after an audit?

You get a prioritised roadmap. You can deliver it yourselves, or we can train your team and build the pieces you need.

Do you only work with agencies?

Agencies and marketing teams are our focus, but we also build websites and tools for other businesses. Get in touch and we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.
