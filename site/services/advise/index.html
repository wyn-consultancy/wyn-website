Questions

What's the difference between the Opportunity Session and the audit?

The Opportunity Session is a fast start: 90 minutes on your biggest opportunities and a written priority list. The audit goes deeper, mapping how your teams actually work and producing a full roadmap with tools, owners and expected outcomes.

Who needs to be involved?

Usually a senior sponsor and a handful of people from the teams in scope. We agree who at kick-off.

What do we get at the end?

A prioritised roadmap: what to do first, which tools to use, who owns what and what to expect from it. Presented in a playback session so your team can ask questions.

Can you help us deliver the roadmap?

Yes. We can train your team and build the pieces you need, or support you month to month as your Fractional AI Lead.
