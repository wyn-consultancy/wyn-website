Questions

Who owns what you build?

You do. Once the project is paid for, what we build for you is yours.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Every build includes handover and team training, and ongoing support is available, either on its own or as part of a Fractional AI Lead arrangement.

Which platforms do you build on?

Whatever fits. Where possible we build on the tools you already use, and write custom code where it's needed.

How long does a build take?

It depends on scope. Small automations can take days; larger tools take a few weeks. You get a timeline alongside the fixed price before we start.
