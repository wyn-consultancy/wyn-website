Questions

How many people can attend a workshop?

Up to 12 people per workshop, so everyone gets hands-on time. For larger groups we run several sessions or a talk followed by smaller workshops.

Remote or in person?

Both. In-person workshops work best for hands-on practice, but remote sessions work well for distributed teams.

Is the training tailored?

Yes. Every workshop is built around your team's real work, briefs and tools, so people practise on tasks they'll do next week.

What do people take away?

A playbook with the workflows and prompts covered in the session, so the learning doesn't stay in the room.
