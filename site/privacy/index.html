Last updated 16 October 2026.

This page explains what personal data WYN Consultancy ("WYN", "we") collects through this website, why, and what you can do about it. If you have any questions, email wyn-consultancy@gmail.com.

WYN Consultancy is an AI consultancy based in London, UK. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Services we use

GitHub Pages (website hosting), Google Fonts (typefaces), Cal.com (bookings), Google Analytics and Meta (only with your consent). Some of these providers process data outside the UK, under safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or adequacy regulations.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and booking details are kept for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the length of the relationship plus any period required for legal or accounting reasons. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, or withdraw consent at any time. Email wyn-consultancy@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
